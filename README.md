# History of 118

## Overview

**118** was a chatpacking community/cult that emerged in the early months of **2025**. According to the community history I know, it was founded by **smxrk, sxyth, and Cyrus**.

118 was active across both **Discord** and **Blockman GO (Blockmango)**. Its main presence was on Discord, while its presence in Blockman GO functioned more as a supportive branch.

I am documenting 118 because I have a strong interest in its history and consider myself a fan and supporter of the community. I was not a member of 118 and was not personally present during the events described below, so this archive should be understood as a community-history account rather than a claim of firsthand participation.

---

## 118's Entry Into Blockman GO

After 118 established a presence in Blockman GO, conflicts developed between 118 and the **SH0T** community.

According to the account of the events, members associated with SH0T attempted to infiltrate the 118 clan. They eventually succeeded, and the clan was subsequently destroyed.

This incident contributed to a larger conflict between the two communities on Discord.

## How the Conflict Progressed

The confrontation initially began as a **3 vs 4** because Zynox was not initially aware that the conflict was taking place.

After Zynox became aware of the conflict and joined, the confrontation became **4 vs 4**.

At one point, the 118 side appeared to have the advantage.

Alex was arguing with Kiler while smxrk started a countdown.

At the same time, Zyvex was involved in a confrontation with Aiden, Nova, and Matrixo. According to the account of the events, Zyvex held his ground and the confrontation was considered either a win or a tie rather than a loss.

Eventually, Alex, smxrk, yuno, and zyvex confronted the SH0T side as a group. According to the account being preserved here, the 118 side ultimately dominated the confrontation.

Afterward, members of the SH0T side reportedly changed their usernames and left the servers involved.

---

# What Happened Afterwards

After the conflict with SH0T, the members of 118 eventually became more occupied with **real-life responsibilities and work**. As activity declined, the 118 community gradually faded.

Despite eventually becoming inactive, 118 is remembered by its supporters as one of the major and most powerful chatpacking communities of its time, with notable members and figures including **smxrk, Alex, yuno, sayth, Cyrus, and zyvex**.

The community's activity across Discord and Blockman GO, along with its conflicts and reputation within the chatpacking scene, became an important part of the history associated with 118.
